## Components under the LGPL

`jcommon`, `jfreechart`, `openpdf`

The LGPL allows redistribution inside a larger work, but it attaches obligations that
Apache-2.0 and MIT do not: recipients must be told the library is LGPL, be able to obtain
its source, and be able to replace it with their own build. Because these sit inside the
fragment jar rather than beside it, satisfying that means pointing at upstream sources in
the release notes. Check this before publishing a release publicly.
